# Code of Conduct

Participation in Harnis project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/): be respectful, constructive and considerate of others.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block participants, at their own discretion.
